# From one study to the whole field

An animated page in fourteen steps: why policy debates need common facts, what an Open Policy Analysis (OPA) is,
how two OPAs compared narrow a debate, one analysis of AI and the economy before and after it was made into an
OPA, and the census of all quantitative analyses of AI and the US economy, scored on a scale of 1 to 7.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or see the published page. The page is self-contained: data, script and styles
are inline.

The page is built in the census project from `proposal/zoom/template.html` and the census data
(`python3 proposal/zoom/build.py`), then copied here. Edit it there, not here.
